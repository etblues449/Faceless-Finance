# Hook, Overlay & Format Patterns

The packaging half of the intelligence layer. Once a moment is selected, this is
how the best podcast teams *frame* it. Use these to write the spoken hook, the
text overlay, and the format recommendation for each clip.

## Spoken hook (the first 3 seconds)

The hook is whatever is said in the first ~3 seconds — sometimes the moment's own
opening line, sometimes a payoff line pulled forward. Patterns, strongest first:

| Pattern | Shape | Example framing |
|---|---|---|
| **Number-forward** | Lead with the surprising figure | "£200 a month became £40,000 in eight years." |
| **Contrarian flip** | "Everyone does X. Do the opposite." | "Everyone saves what's left. That's exactly why they're broke." |
| **Named mistake** | "The biggest mistake with X is…" | "The biggest mistake people make with money…" |
| **Curiosity gap** | Name the payoff, withhold the how | "One habit turned my finances around — here's the habit." |
| **Direct challenge** | Second person, stakes | "You're saving wrong, and it's costing you thousands." |
| **Confession** | Guest reveals something costly/personal | "I lost £X before I learned this." |
| **Question cold-open** | The exact question the viewer has | "Why do most people stay broke on a good salary?" |

Rules: front-load; no throat-clearing ("so", "basically", "I think"); one idea;
say the concrete thing, not the category. If the raw moment buries its hook,
recommend **payoff-forward** — quote the line to move and where it goes.

## Text overlay (on-screen title)

The overlay is the silent hook — most feeds autoplay muted. It should stand alone.

- **≤ ~7 words**, front-loaded, sentence or title case (match the account).
- Usually a compression of the `quotable_line`, not a caption of the first line.
- Create a **curiosity gap** the clip then closes; don't spoil the payoff in the
  overlay unless the format is payoff-forward.
- Give 2–3 options per clip. Common molds:
  - *Stat:* "£200 → £40,000 in 8 years"
  - *Contrarian:* "Save FIRST, spend second"
  - *Mistake:* "The #1 money mistake"
  - *Question:* "Why good salaries stay broke"
  - *List tease:* "The one habit that did it"

Keep it distinct from burned-in **captions** (the running subtitles). Overlay =
one persistent title; captions = the spoken words, always on for muted viewing.

## Format recommendation

Pick one and justify it from the moment's nature:

| Format | Use when | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Talking-head + captions** | Default; a single strong speaker delivering | Tightest, cheapest, travels well. |
| **Captioned static / quote card** | One killer line, little visual motion | Overlay does the work; good for a pure aphorism. |
| **Split-screen / reaction** | Host↔guest tension or a big reaction | Keep both faces; the dynamic is the content. |
| **B-roll over VO** | Concrete nouns/numbers to visualise | Use sparingly — face-time retains better on podcasts. |
| **Payoff-forward cut** | Weak open, strong late payoff | Cold-open the payoff line, then play it straight. |

For this channel specifically, honour `CHARACTER_BIBLE.md` (one consistent
presenter) and the muted-first, captions-always default.

## Edit instructions (what you hand the editor)

Make them cut-ready and unambiguous:

- **In / out:** tightened to the second (not the raw window boundary).
- **Context to cut:** the exact setup/preamble lines removed.
- **Payoff-forward move:** which line moves to the front, if any.
- **Captions:** always on, burned, from real timings; emphasise the hook + the number.
- **Emphasis beats:** where to punch in, freeze, or overlay the title.
- **Overlay timing:** title in by 0.5s, hold through the hook, drop before the payoff.

Example:
> In 00:00:31 ("Here's the biggest mistake…"), out 00:00:57 (end on "…nothing to
> do with income"). Cut the 00:00:00–00:00:30 small-talk entirely. Payoff-forward:
> lift "£200 a month into £40,000 in eight years" to a 0–3s cold open, then play
> the clip straight. Overlay option 2 ("Save FIRST, spend second") in at 0.5s.
> Captions on throughout; punch in on "20 percent" and "£40,000".
